# ELIMUCOIN - Go-Live Overview (No Timeline)

## What This Is (Plain English)

ELIMUCOIN is a decentralized university where people learn skills, earn $OMNI tokens, and prove those skills with permanent credentials. It connects education, income, and community governance in one system. The goal is to deploy it live, onboard real learners, and run it as a working product.

---

## What Already Exists in the Codebase

Core building blocks are present and ready for integration and deployment:

- Smart contracts: $OMNI token, soulbound credentials, learn-to-earn rewards, curriculum DAO, venture DAO.
- AI services: AI Sensei (tutor) and Opportunity Radar (job matching).
- Integrations: M-Pesa bridge, GoMyCode curriculum sync, biometric verification.
- Physical nodes: Raspberry Pi deployment script, solar node manager.
- Documentation: architecture, deployment, walkthrough, and summaries.

---

## How It Works (Simple Flow)

1. A user signs up with their phone (M-Pesa) and verifies identity.
2. They choose a course and start learning.
3. AI Sensei helps with tutoring, quizzes, and explanations.
4. Completing modules and projects earns $OMNI rewards.
5. The user gets a soulbound credential as proof of skill.
6. They can cash out to M-Pesa or stake tokens to join a guild.
7. Opportunity Radar matches them to jobs and gigs.
8. $OMNI holders vote on courses, budgets, and community decisions.
