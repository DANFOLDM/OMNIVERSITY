# ELIMUCOIN: The Story of Africa's Decentralized University

## 🎯 The Big Idea (In Simple Terms)

Imagine if learning paid you money, your skills became like gold coins you could spend, and students could run their own universities. That's ELIMUCOIN - a complete system where education becomes a wealth-building machine.

This walkthrough tells the story of how ELIMUCOIN works, from start to finish, in a way your friends can understand.

## 📱 The Complete User Journey

### Day 1: Getting Started

**Step 1: Sign Up (5 minutes)**
You download the app (iOS/Android) or visit the website. Enter your M-Pesa phone number (+254 7XX XXX XXX), get an SMS code, create a password, and agree to terms. Boom - you're in!

**Step 2: Verify Your Identity (2 minutes)**
For security, you verify who you are using biometrics:
- Face recognition: Position your face in the camera
- Fingerprint: Place finger on sensor
- Voice: Say a phrase
- Or use Worldcoin if you have it

**Step 3: Create Your Profile (5 minutes)**
Tell the system about yourself:
- Name, age, location (Nairobi, Kenya)
- Education level (high school, some college)
- Learning goals (become a web developer, data scientist)
- Preferred language (English, Swahili)
- Study time per week (10 hours)

**Step 4: Welcome Bonus!**
You get 100 $OMNI tokens as a welcome gift, plus a "Welcome Learner" soulbound credential (like a permanent digital badge). Your wallet shows: 100 OMNI ≈ 10,000 KES.

## 🏗️ How It All Works Together

### The 7 Layers of ELIMUCOIN

1. **Blockchain Layer**: Smart contracts handle money, credentials, and voting
2. **Token Layer**: $OMNI powers everything - rewards, staking, governance
3. **AI Layer**: Sensei teaches, Radar finds jobs
4. **Education Layer**: GoMyCode provides courses, we add rewards
5. **Finance Layer**: M-Pesa connects to real money
6. **Community Layer**: Guilds let students work together
7. **Physical Layer**: Solar nodes bring internet to rural areas

### Key Innovations

- **Learn-to-Earn**: Every learning activity earns tokens
- **Soulbound Credentials**: Permanent proof of skills
- **Decentralized Governance**: Community votes on everything
- **Guild Economy**: Students form companies and split profits
- **Biometric Security**: Multiple ways to verify identity
- **Offline Learning**: Solar-powered nodes work without internet
